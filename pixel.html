PIXEL

A picture chip on the same memory as the sound chip. Every setting is a byte. Each voice of the song has a visual meaning, so you can see the music: kick is brightness, snare is warmth, lead is pattern detail, bass is zoom, noise is sparkle.

SAFE is on in every preset: at most 3 flashes a second, softer flashes (WCAG 2.3.1). It is bit 1 of $2000.

Pick a preset, then PLAY to hear the song and see the beat.
